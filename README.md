# Polyglossia Praxis

Welcome to **Polyglossia Praxis**, your personal digital companion for mastering the ancient languages of Greek, Hebrew, and Latin. This application combines powerful lexical tools with AI-driven learning games to create an immersive and effective study environment.

## Core Features

Polyglossia Praxis is organized into two main sections: comprehensive **Lexicon Tools** for deep-diving into language specifics, and interactive **Learning Games** to reinforce your vocabulary and grammar skills.

---

### 🏛️ Greek Tools

A full suite of tools for students of Ancient Greek.

-   **Greek Lexicon**: A central hub for word analysis. Look up any Greek word to see its morphological breakdown, dictionary entries from major lexicons (LSJ, Thayer, Dodson, Strong's), and all its occurrences throughout the Greek New Testament and the Septuagint (LXX).
-   **Greek Prepositions Guide**: An interactive module to learn and quiz yourself on the various meanings of Greek prepositions depending on the case they govern.
-   **Greek Syntax Guide**: A comprehensive reference for common syntactic structures in Ancient Greek, including conditional clauses, participles, infinitives, and more, complete with clear examples.
-   **Vocabulary Browser**: Manage and browse your saved vocabulary lists. You can review words you've previously looked up and saved.

---

### 📜 Hebrew Tools

Specialized tools designed for students of Biblical Hebrew.

-   **Hebrew Lexicon**: Look up Hebrew words or Strong's numbers to get detailed entries from the Brown-Driver-Briggs (BDB) lexicon and Strong's Concordance, along with a full list of every occurrence in the Hebrew Bible.
-   **Hebrew Morph Builder**: A powerful tool for advanced students. Construct detailed MorphHB (Hebrew Bible Morphology) codes to find every instance of a specific grammatical form in the text.
-   **Vowel Reconstruction (AI)**: An innovative AI-powered tool that takes a Biblical Hebrew word and traces it back to its reconstructed Proto-Semitic root, explaining the historical sound changes along the way.

---

### 🏛️ Latin Tools

Essential resources for studying Classical Latin.

-   **Latin Lexicon**: Look up Latin words to view their definitions and morphological analysis from respected sources like Lewis & Short.
-   **Whitaker's Words Tool**: Get quick and comprehensive morphological analysis for any Latin word using the renowned Whitaker's Words parsing engine.

---

### 🎮 Learning Games & AI Features

Reinforce your knowledge with interactive games and AI-powered practice tools.

-   **Matching Game**: A fun, fast-paced game to test your vocabulary. Match words to their meanings against the clock. This game supports Greek, Hebrew, and Latin and allows you to create custom word sets.
-   **Flashcard Game**: Review vocabulary using a classic flashcard format. Flip cards to reveal meanings, and use various word banks to customize your study sessions.
-   **Parser Game**: Hone your grammar skills by parsing Greek verbs, nouns, and participles. The game challenges you to identify tense, voice, mood, case, and more.
-   **AI Story Creator**: A unique, AI-driven feature to bring your vocabulary to life.
    -   **Generate Stories**: Create original, simple stories in Ancient Greek using words from your saved vocabulary lists.
    -   **Text-to-Speech Audio**: Listen to your generated story with AI-narrated audio, using Modern Greek pronunciation for consistency.
    -   **Synchronized Highlighting**: Follow along as the player highlights each word in real-time as it's spoken. Click any word to jump to that point in the audio.
    -   **Looping & Practice**: Loop the full audio or select a specific phrase to repeat for focused listening practice.
    -   **Pronunciation Check**: Record yourself speaking a phrase and get instant AI feedback on your pronunciation.
    -   **Offline Favorites**: Save your favorite stories for full offline access, including audio playback.
    -   **Manual Story Upload**: Add your own text and audio files to use with the story player and timing tools.
